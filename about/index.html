About StraightGate Finance

StraightGate is a small independent publisher of spreadsheet tools and plain-language guides for people who earn money through platforms: freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr, online tutors on Preply, italki and Cambly, self-published authors on Amazon KDP, and people selling second-hand items on Vinted, eBay, Wallapop, Leboncoin, Subito or Kleinanzeigen.

What we do

We answer one concrete money question per page – “how much do I actually keep?”, “when will the platform report me?” – with a direct answer, a worked numeric example, a comparison table and links to the official source for every rule or fee.

How we research

  • Rules and fees come from official sources: EU law on EUR-Lex, national tax authorities (BZSt, Agencia Tributaria, impots.gouv.fr, Agenzia delle Entrate) and each platform's own help or fee pages.
  • Every page shows the date it was last checked. Examples use illustrative numbers and are labelled as such.
  • We don't give tax or legal advice; we explain the published rules and give you a tool to check your own numbers.

How we make money

Each guide links a free spreadsheet and a paid full version, sold on Gumroad, Payhip, and Creem (selected tools). We are not affiliated with or paid by any platform mentioned.

Contact and corrections

Spotted an outdated fee or rule? Message us on X at @StraightGateFin and we will re-check the source and update the page.
