Preply vs italki vs Cambly: how much do online tutors keep after commission?

Last updated: 2026-10-19 · By the StraightGate Finance team

Short answer: From a $25 one-hour lesson, a tutor keeps $16.75–$20.50 on Preply (commission falls from 33% to 18% as your hours grow, and every first trial with a new student is kept 100% by Preply), $19.75 on italki for a single lesson (21%; packages 15–19%, trials 0%), while Cambly doesn't use your price at all and pays $10.20 per hour of active chat ($0.17 per minute).

Worked example

Illustrative numbers, not a real person's data.

A tutor teaches 40 regular hours in one month at $25/hour ($1,000 paid by students). Assumptions: on Preply she already has 60 lifetime hours (25% tier); on italki all lessons are single lessons; on Cambly she has 40 hours of active chat. Withdrawal to PayPal: Preply 2% (max $20); italki has no fee of its own and PayPal's charge (~2%) varies by country.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Preply take 100% of the trial lesson?

Preply charges a 100% commission on the first trial lesson with each new student; after that, regular lessons are charged 18–33% depending on your total hours taught on Preply.

Does italki charge commission on trial lessons?

No. italki charges 0% on trial lessons, 21% on single lessons, 15–19% on packages and 30% on group classes.

Does Cambly pay for waiting time?

No. Cambly pays $0.17 per minute of active chat for adult students ($0.20 for Cambly Kids); time waiting for calls is unpaid.

Which platform pays tutors the most?

It depends on your price and how many new students you get. With a high hourly price, italki packages or Verbling keep the largest share; with many new students Preply's 100% trial commission matters; Cambly is fixed pay regardless of experience.

Are withdrawal fees included?

Not in the per-lesson table. Preply charges e.g. 2% (max $20) to PayPal; italki charges no fee itself, but PayPal, Payoneer and banks do.
