What does PayPal charge when a client abroad pays you?

Last updated: 2026-10-19 · By the StraightGate Finance team

Short answer: On PayPal's US fee page, receiving money for goods and services costs 2.99%, and PayPal adds 1.50% when the payer is in another country. If PayPal then converts the money into another currency, it applies a currency conversion spread of 3.00% (4.00% for some payment types) – so an international payment can cost around 7–8% in total before any withdrawal fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 1.50% international fee charged on top of 2.99%?

Yes. PayPal says the domestic receiving rate applies plus the additional percentage-based fee for international commercial transactions.

Can I avoid the 3% conversion spread?

Only by not letting PayPal convert – for example by keeping the balance in that currency or withdrawing through a route that converts at a better rate. Whether that is possible depends on your country and account.

Do these rates apply in my country?

This page quotes PayPal's United States fee page. PayPal has a fee page for every market; rates and fixed fees differ.
