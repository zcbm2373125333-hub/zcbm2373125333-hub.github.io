Does Preply really keep 100% of the trial lesson – and what does a new tutor earn?

Last updated: 2026-10-19 · By the StraightGate Finance team

Short answer: Yes. Preply keeps 100% of the first trial lesson with every new student; after that it charges 33% of your hourly rate for your first 20 hours, falling in steps to 18% after 400 hours. In a new tutor's first month, that can mean keeping less than half of what students paid.

Worked example

Illustrative numbers, not a real person's data.

A new tutor charges $20/hour. In her first month she gives 6 one-hour trial lessons (one per new student); 4 students continue and book 14 hours of regular lessons. Her lifetime total is then 20 hours, so the 33% tier applies all month.

Students pay in total$400.00 (20 h × $20)
Trials: 6 × $20, commission 100%$0.00 to the tutor
Regular: 14 × $20 = $280, commission 33%$187.60 to the tutor
Kept$187.60 = 46.9% of what students paid

Month 2, same price, 20 regular hours and no new trials: her hours 21–40 fall in the 28% tier, so she keeps $288.00 of $400 (72%).

Frequently asked questions

Is the trial commission 100% for every trial?

It applies to the first trial lesson with each new student. Lessons after that are charged at your current tier.

Does the commission ever reach 0% on Preply?

No. The lowest tier is 18%, after 400 hours.

How can a new tutor reduce the impact?

Track how many trials convert into regular students. A low conversion rate costs more than the commission tier itself, because every unconverted trial earns $0.
