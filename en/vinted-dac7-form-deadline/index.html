Vinted asked me to fill in the DAC7 form – what is the deadline and what if I don't?

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · By the StraightGate Finance team · Other languages: Deutsch

Short answer: Vinted asks for the DAC7 form once you complete 30 sales or sell more than €2,000 in a calendar year. According to Vinted's help centre, it is due by early January of the following year or within 60 days of reaching the threshold, whichever is later; if you miss it, Vinted blocks your Vinted wallet and then your selling until you submit it. Filling it in does not by itself mean you owe tax.

Worked example

Illustrative numbers, not a real person's data.

  • 30th sale on 1 March 2026 → form due by early January 2027.
  • 30th sale on 15 December 2026 → 60 days apply, so the form is due by about 13 February 2027.
  • Missed deadline → no wallet payouts or wallet payments; one more sale is possible, then selling is switched off. Submitting the form restores access.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay tax after filling in the DAC7 form?

Not because of the form. Vinted states that selling your own personal items is generally not taxed; tax questions depend on your country and whether you buy to resell.

Does a bundle count as one sale?

Vinted counts completed sales (transactions); check your sales count in the app, since rules for counting can be platform-specific.

Do sales on other platforms count toward Vinted's 30?

No. DAC7 thresholds apply per platform.

Are the deadlines the same in every country?

This page quotes Vinted's German help page; check the help page for your country's Vinted site.
