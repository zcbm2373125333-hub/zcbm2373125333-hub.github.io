KDP 70% vs 35% eBook royalty: which pays more for my book?

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · By the StraightGate Finance team

Short answer: KDP's 70% option pays 70% × (list price − VAT − delivery cost) in eligible territories, while the 35% option pays 35% × (list price − VAT) with no delivery cost. For normal-sized eBooks priced in the 70% range, 70% pays roughly double – but for very large files (lots of images) the delivery cost can make 70% pay less than 35%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum price for the 70% royalty?

Since 7 July 2026 it is $12.99 on Amazon.com (up from $9.99), with equivalent ceilings such as €12.99 and £12.99 in other marketplaces. Existing books are not switched automatically: a book priced $9.99–$12.99 on the 35% option has to be changed to 70% in KDP.

When does the 35% option pay more?

When delivery cost is large relative to price – roughly when the file is many megabytes and the price is low. Compute both formulas for your file size.

Does VAT reduce my royalty?

Yes. Both formulas use the price excluding VAT, so EU sales earn less than the headline price suggests.

What happens if Amazon price-matches my book?

Your royalty is calculated on the lower price Amazon sells at; if a price-match drops the price below the 70% minimum, you earn 35%.
