How do I calculate a translation quote from a Trados or memoQ analysis with fuzzy-match discounts?

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · By the StraightGate Finance team · Other languages: 日本語

Short answer: Take the word count of each match band from your CAT analysis (Trados Studio, memoQ, Phrase), multiply it by the percentage of your full rate the client pays for that band, add the bands up to get your weighted words, and multiply by your per-word rate. If the result is below your minimum fee, charge the minimum. The pay percentages are not an industry standard – each agency uses its own grid – so ask for the grid before you accept the job.

Three things that change the final number

Minimum fee. On small jobs the weighted quote can fall below your minimum fee. Compare the two and charge the higher one.

Character-based source languages. For Chinese or Japanese source text, Trados counts characters, not words (RWS). Use a per-character rate in exactly the same formula.

What actually reaches you. The quote is not what lands in your account. PayPal, Wise and Payoneer can each take a percentage fee, a fixed fee and a currency-conversion margin, and rates differ by country – see our guide to PayPal fees on international payments and PayPal's fee page. Track the net amount per client, not just the invoice total.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a standard fuzzy-match discount grid?

No. Each agency sets its own pay percentages per band, and both Trados and memoQ let the bands themselves be configured. Ask for the grid (it is often in the PO or vendor agreement) before accepting work.

Should repetitions be paid?

That depends on the client's grid. CAT tools report repetitions as a separate line, so check how your client prices them – many grids treat them like 100% matches, but don't assume.

How do I quote per character for Japanese or Chinese source text?

Trados counts characters for character-based languages. Put the character counts per band into the same formula and use your per-character rate.

What if the weighted quote is below my minimum fee?

Charge the minimum fee. Agree on the minimum with each client up front so it is not a surprise on the invoice.

Can I do this in Google Sheets?

Yes. Our free calculator works in Excel and Google Sheets: type the grid, paste the word counts per band and it shows the weighted words, the quote and the discount you are giving.
