StraightGate Finance – Guides

  • How do I calculate a translation quote from a Trados or memoQ analysis with fuzzy-match discounts?
    Multiply each match band's words by the client's pay %, add them up (weighted words) and multiply by your rate. Worked 5,000-word example + free calculator.
  • Will Vinted, eBay or Wallapop report me to the tax office? The DAC7 30-sales / €2,000 rule explained
    Under EU DAC7, a platform reports you if you make 30+ sales or receive more than €2,000 on that platform in a year. Per-platform counting, worked example, what is reported, and a free checker.
  • Is the DAC7 reporting threshold changing to €3,000 with no 30-sales limit?
    The European Commission's COM(2026) 308 proposal would drop the 30-sale test and raise the DAC7 threshold to €3,000 from 2028. What changes, what doesn't, and examples.
  • How much do freelancers actually keep from a $1,000 Upwork or Fiverr job?
    Fiverr keeps 20% of each order; Upwork's Freelancer Service Fee is 0–15% per contract. Worked $1,000 example including currency conversion, comparison table and a free fee calculator.
  • What does PayPal charge when a client abroad pays you?
    On PayPal's US fee page, receiving goods-and-services money costs 2.99%, plus 1.50% when the sender is abroad, plus a 3–4% conversion spread. Worked example and comparison table.
  • Preply vs italki vs Cambly: how much do online tutors keep after commission?
    Preply takes 33%→18% (and 100% of trials), italki 0–30% by lesson type, Cambly pays $0.17 per minute. Side-by-side table for a $25 lesson, worked monthly example, free calculator.
  • Does Preply really keep 100% of the trial lesson – and what does a new tutor earn?
    Yes: Preply's commission is 100% on the first trial lesson with each new student, then 33% falling to 18%. Worked first-month example for a $20/h tutor and a free calculator.
  • What is my break-even ACOS on Amazon KDP ads?
    Break-even ACOS = royalty per sale ÷ list price. A $4.99 eBook at 70% breaks even at about 68% ACOS; a $12.99 paperback with $3.50 printing at about 33%. Formula, table, free calculator.
  • KDP 70% vs 35% eBook royalty: which pays more for my book?
    70% royalty = 70% × (price − VAT − delivery cost); 35% = 35% × (price − VAT). For large files the 70% option can pay less. Worked examples from KDP's own rules.
  • Why is my Upwork fee 15% – and who pays less?
    Since 1 May 2025 Upwork's Freelancer Service Fee is variable, 0–15% per new contract. Exceptions (Any Hire, Direct Contracts, Payroll), gross-up formula and worked examples.
  • Vinted asked me to fill in the DAC7 form – what is the deadline and what if I don't?
    Vinted contacts you after 30 sales or €2,000+ in a year. The form is due by early January or within 60 days, whichever is later; otherwise wallet and selling are blocked.
  • How much fostering income is tax-free in 2025-26 (qualifying care relief)?
    2025-26 limit: £19,690 per household plus £415 a week per child under 11 and £495 for 11 or over. 2026-27: £20,440, £435, £515. Worked example.
  • Is the UK side-hustle tax limit £1,000 or £3,000?
    Still £1,000. Gross trading income over £1,000 means registering for Self Assessment. The £3,000 figure is a planned reporting threshold with no start date – and it is not tax-free.
