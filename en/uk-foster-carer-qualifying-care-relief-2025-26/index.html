How much fostering income is tax-free in 2025-26 (qualifying care relief)?

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · By the StraightGate Finance team

Short answer: Under HMRC's qualifying care relief, your tax-free limit for 2025-26 is a fixed amount of £19,690 per household plus a weekly amount for each placement: £415 for a child under 11 and £495 for a child aged 11 or over (or an adult). If your total care receipts are at or below that limit, there is no Income Tax or Class 4 NIC on your caring income; for 2026-27 the amounts rise to £20,440, £435 and £515.

How the weeks are counted

  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday, and a part-week counts as a full week. A stay from Friday to Monday spans two weeks, so it counts as 2 (HS236).
  • The rate changes in the week of the 11th birthday, so a placement that crosses a birthday is split between the two rates.
  • The fixed amount is shared equally between carers in the household and is reduced pro rata if you were approved for only part of the year (BIM52765).
  • Respite and short breaks count as placements for the weeks they cover.

If your receipts are above the limit

You can choose the simplified method (taxable profit = receipts minus the qualifying amount) or the normal profit method (receipts minus actual expenses and capital allowances), whichever gives the lower figure. HS236 says to complete a Self Assessment return and claim the relief on the self-employment (short) pages, even when your receipts are under the limit (GOV.UK).

Frequently asked questions

Does a part week count as a full week?

Yes. HMRC counts weeks Monday to Sunday and a part-week counts in full, so a Friday-to-Monday stay counts as 2 weeks.

My partner and I both foster. Do we each get £19,690?

No. The fixed amount is per household and is shared equally between the carers, so with two carers each gets £9,845 for 2025-26. Weekly amounts go to the carer who receives the payments for that placement.

I was only approved partway through the year. What changes?

The fixed amount is reduced in proportion to the part of the tax year you were approved for. The weekly amounts still apply to every week you had a placement.

Do I need to file a tax return if I'm under the limit?

HMRC's HS236 says to fill in a Self Assessment return and claim qualifying care relief on the self-employment (short) pages. The return for 2025-26 is due online by 31 January 2027.

What are the amounts for 2026-27?

£20,440 fixed per household, £435 a week for a child under 11 and £515 for a child aged 11 or over or an adult, set by SI 2026/39.
